# Privacy Policy: Subtitle Overlay

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

Subtitle Overlay is a Chrome extension that shows subtitles from a file you choose on top of web videos.

## Data we collect

**None.** Subtitle Overlay does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

## What the extension stores, and where

- **The subtitle file you load** (its file name and subtitle lines) and **your settings** (size, color, timing, position and similar) are saved only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own computer.
- This data never leaves your computer. The extension makes no network requests and has no servers.
- You can delete it at any time by choosing **Remove subtitle** in the extension's popup, or by uninstalling the extension.

## Access to websites

The extension runs on the pages you visit so it can find the video on the page and draw subtitles over it. It does not read, record or send the content of those pages, your browsing history, or anything you type.

## Third parties

No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services are used.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
